WHO WE ARE

Our roots are in the ocean.

Somafish is an online seafood shop based in Mogadishu, Somalia. Our story began in 1997, with a simple idea: make good seafood easier to find and bring home.

Today, we serve households and businesses looking for fresh, frozen and chilled seafood, with care given to handling and delivery.
